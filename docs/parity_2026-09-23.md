## Findings

### F1 — Below-ground statics show through the ground (regression reported by the user)

**Fixed 2026-09-25** with option (b): `RenderLists.CoverFromBelow` queues the land in front of
a sunk static/multi/item into the sorted pass, drawn from its own baked quad. ADR-0004 amendment
2026-09-25. Verified with `side_by_side` + `render_diff` at britain-street and a GUO reshoot of
all eight places (`ab_compare --only guo`); the plinth is gone and nothing else moved.


- **Where:** britain-street and britain-street-night. The T-shaped grey-roof building's stone
  foundation (0x001B/0x001C, flooring 0x04B5 at z=-20/-21 under 1607..1611,1590..1591) shows
  as a plinth under the timber walls. ClassicUO draws the walls meeting the street.
- **Cause (traced by the GUO-Fable session, read-only):** `godot/GUO/src/Game/Scenes/RenderLists.cs`
  `DrawRenderLists` draws every baked land mesh first, then `_tiles`/`_stretchedTiles`, then
  meshed statics, then the CPU-sorted world list. Land never takes part in the depth sort, and
  the batcher paints in submission order. ClassicUO hides these pieces with the depth buffer:
  the z=-20 pieces land on screen where the z=0 street tiles in front of them win per pixel.
  `ChunkMesh.IsStaticExcludedFromMesh` sends all statics through the sorted pass, after all land,
  so a static below the ground in front of it is always painted over that ground.
- **Not caused by** the profile (`highlight_game_objects`, etc.) or Razor: none of those changes
  list membership or order. 43f95e7 rebuilt today shows the same plinth.
- **Open question:** `build/screenshots/ab/britain-street/guo_2026-09-21.png` (09-21 20:33) is
  clean, although the same code draws the plinth today. Not explained. Candidates: a dirty tree
  at the time, or a different player position changing the screen cull.
- **Fix direction (needs an ADR-0004 amendment; a decision for the morning):**
  (a) route land through the sorted list (loses the mesh win for land);
  (b) keep the bake but pull out of it any land tile whose chunk has a static with Z below the
  tile's Z, and sort those;
  (c) drop statics whose top (Z + tiledata height) is at or below the ground Z of the tile in
  front of them. Cheapest and matches what the depth buffer does here, but it is a heuristic
  and needs the A/B tool on more places.

### F2 — Ground missing at the viewport edges

- **Where:** britain-coast. GUO leaves black triangles along the left edge over the water
  (about y=1290 in the sheet) and a black sawtooth along the top edge above the grass on the
  right. ClassicUO fills both.
- **Suspected path:** not the per-tile cull. `_minPixel`/`_maxPixel` in
  `GameSceneDrawingSorting.cs` (lines 570, 769, 787) are identical to upstream. More likely the
  set of chunks whose land mesh is drawn (`visibleChunks` in `RenderLists.DrawRenderLists`) is
  tighter than the tiles ClassicUO draws at the border. Unverified.

### F3 — Trees drawn over roofs in front of them

- **Where:** britain-street (east house, circled by the user) and britain-shop (the tree
  over the thatched roof at the bottom centre). ClassicUO keeps the trees behind the roofs.
- **Suspected path:** both are statics in the same sorted list, so this is not F1's land
  ordering. Likely `CalculateDepthZ`/`PriorityZ` for roof tiles versus foliage, or the
  roof/foliage branch in `GameSceneDrawingSorting.cs` (~963-975). Not traced.

### F4 — Small object drawn on top of a roof

- **Where:** britain-shop, the long thatched roof on the right: a small grey object sits on
  the roof in GUO only. Probably the same class as F3 (something behind or under the roof
  sorted after it). Needs a closer look before it is counted separately.

### F5 — "It begins to rain" in GUO only (low priority, added 2026-09-28)

- **Where:** the B7 visual A/B runs (`tools/visual_ab`, pass 2, Britain): GUO's shots show

### F6 — A deep multi's foot shows through the land in front of it (added 2026-09-29)

- **Where:** the tools/multi tower test (a tower from z -128 up through the void's z 0 land,
  near 6146,1258), zoomed out to 2.5. GUO draws the bottom wall course, 128 below the land,
  as a lone piece far down the screen; ClassicUO hides it. At 0.5 and 1.0 the piece is off
  screen and the two match. Frames: `build/tower_test/_ab_whole25.png`, `_bottom_piece3x.png`.
- **Not the view cull.** `_minPixel`/`_maxPixel`, the tile range and `Camera` are identical
  to upstream (Camera's Transform2D is the same matrix chain). ClassicUO hides the piece with
  its depth buffer: the z 0 land about 12 tiles in front paints over it per pixel.
- **Cause:** F1's `RenderLists.CoverFromBelow` redraws the land in front of a sunk object, but
  capped at 8 tiles on each axis -- 16 rows, about 77 z down the screen. The tower's middle
  courses are covered by land other components queue; the front course hangs further than any.
- **Fix (`work/f6-deep-cover`):** the 8-tile square is unchanged; past it, the tiles down the
  diagonal to the sprite's foot and across its width are queued as well. Not yet A/B'd.
- **"Texture not found" in the same runs is parity:** ClassicUO logs the same lines in the same
  runs (paperdoll gump 60000 once; items 0xFFE0-0xFFE6 every frame), so they are not GUO's.
