GodotUO 0.1.1 is a performance update to the 0.1.0 preview. Everything in 0.1.0 still applies: it is
pre-release software, and **you need your own Ultima Online installation**. Nothing in this release
contains game data.

## Downloads

| File | For | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `GUO-windows-x86_64.zip` | Windows 10/11, x64 | Unzip and run `GUO.exe`. |
| `GUO-steamdeck-linux-x86_64.zip` | Steam Deck (SteamOS), Linux x86_64 | See the Steam Deck page in the wiki. |
| `GUO-android-arm64-debug.zip` | Android arm64 | A debug build; see the 0.1.0 notes for connecting it to a shard. |

## What changed

- **Faster on the Steam Deck and other Linux machines with OpenGL.** Ground and wall tiles that sit
  next to each other are now drawn together. Zoomed far out on a Steam Deck, most places draw 25 to
  47 percent faster than in 0.1.0.
- **Less work per frame when nothing on screen changes.** On the OpenGL renderer the client now keeps
  the drawing it sent last frame and resends only what changed. On a Steam Deck that is 3 to 11
  percent faster in most places, with pictures identical to before. `GUO_RETAIN_CANVAS=0` turns it
  off.
- **Lighting speed mode is the realtime lighting.** The older realtime lights option, our first
  attempt, cost 10 to 21 milliseconds a frame on a Steam Deck zoomed out and has been retired. Lighting
  speed mode, which needs the default Depth World renderer, replaces it; it adds about 1 to 2
  milliseconds a frame on a Steam Deck. On the OpenGL renderer the game uses the classic lighting.
- **Window light in lighting speed mode** is worked out once per building and kept, saving 1 to 2
  milliseconds a frame on a Steam Deck in towns.
- Smaller fixes to the test and build tooling.

## Known issues

See the Known Issues page in the wiki. On very old integrated graphics the frame rate is now level with
ClassicUO rather than ahead of it: those machines are limited by how many pixels they can draw. The
other 0.1.0 known issues still apply.
