# Brand artwork: provenance and credits

## The GUO sigil (current)

`design/brand/guo-sigil.png` is the master of every icon, the boot splash and the README emblem
(`tools/brand` builds them). It is the project's own mark, released with the repository under its
BSD 2-Clause licence.

## The "Godot face" mark (historical, press kit v1)

Press kit v1 (commit 9d3ebf4, removed from the tree in adf622b "Keep the press kit out of the repo for
now") included `guo-godot-mark-*`: a separately generated "custom Godot face" in engraved silver and gold
with emerald eyes. It remains in the repository's history.

That face is a derivative of the **Godot Engine logo by Andrea Calabró**, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (see the
[Godot press kit](https://godotengine.org/press/)). This file is the credit the licence requires.
Changes: restyled as an engraved metal emblem with gold trim and emerald eyes; not endorsed by the Godot
Foundation.

The current builds don't use the Godot face; they use the GUO sigil above.

## Other artwork

- Built-in background loops, screensavers and Store sample packs: procedural, CC0
  (`godot/GUO/assets/backgrounds/LICENSE.md`, `godot/GUO/assets/screensavers/LICENSE.md`,
  `tools/asset_store/samples.py`).
- Ultima Online game art is never included; players supply their own installation.
