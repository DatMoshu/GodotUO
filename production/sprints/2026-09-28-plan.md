Status: **draft for discussion** (the owner replies in the terminal). Nothing here is dispatched yet.

behind it.

## Where we are

- The port is at 100% by file count (2 verbatim files left). Parity sweeps match ClassicUO across towns,
  dungeons, facets and night lighting, with one recurring exception: **frame rate**.
- Platforms: Windows, Android (dual and single screen), Steam Deck (to login) and the browser (plays in
  Chrome) all build from one repo; CI makes the Windows, Deck and Android downloads.
- Tools: editor phases 0–6, the Asset Store, screensavers, the splash, the wiki, and a release checklist.

## Rules carried forward

At least 16 GB free before a heavy job, one at a time. Android devices asleep when idle. The director is
the only #bot-hub watcher. Desktop stays 1:1 with ClassicUO; mobile features are touch-only. Every ported
edit is marked (CI strict). Nobody but the director merges or pushes.
