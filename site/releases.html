Which download?

  • Windows: choose the windows-x86_64 ZIP and extract the entire folder.
  • Steam Deck / Linux: choose the steamdeck-linux-x86_64 ZIP.
  • Android: release ZIPs contain the APK. A download marked debug is a test build; its signing key may change between builds.

Only files attached to a release are installable builds. GitHub’s automatically generated “Source code” archives are for developers. SHA256SUMS.txt lists checksums for the attached ZIPs.

Development builds

Integration builds are for testing unfinished changes. They expire after seven days and may require a GitHub sign-in to download. They are not published releases.
